02

Material differences

The six fields below are the entire delta. Two of them — cashback settlement timing and the new 4i(5) wording — change the underlying SQL logic and audit trail.

Field Q1 Asia Q2 Asia
Promotion deposit period 01 Jan – 31 Mar 2026 01 Apr – 30 Jun 2026 Different participant universe — anyone signing up in April is in Q2, not Q1.
Trading lot calc. period 01 Jan – 30 Jun 2026 01 Apr – 30 Sep 2026 Q2's lot window extends to end of September.
No-prior-deposit anchor (4c) No deposit prior to 1 January 2026 No deposit prior to 1 April 2026 Different anchor date for first-ever-deposit eligibility.
Cashback settlement (Cl. 5) Major change Within 10 working days Settled immediately Q2 settles instantly. Q2-spillover from Q1 redemptions still follows the Q1 10-day rule; new Q2 redemptions do not.
Position rule 4i(5) New rule Hedging orders etc. not counted Only trades opened and closed within the Promotion Period count. Positions opened before but closed during the period are excluded. Trade-timestamp filter must be tightened on both ends — this changes the SQL.
Lot definition footnote Total closed lots Total closed lots (those that fulfilled clause (i) below) Clause (i) qualification now explicitly enforced.

04

Recommendation

Position

Raise a new, separate Jira ticket for Q2 Asia. Keep PDSPT-265 scoped strictly to Q1.

  1. 1 Different participant pool. 2026_Q1_Asia and 2026_Q2_Asia are distinct CRM campaigns with non-overlapping new active client lists.
  2. 2 Different T&C rules. Immediate cashback settlement (Cl. 5) and the new 4i(5) "opened & closed in period" rule require different SQL filters from Q1.
  3. 3 Different time windows. Mixing Q1 monthly columns (Jan/Feb/Mar) with Q2 monthly columns (Apr/May/Jun) in one delivery would muddy the audit trail.
  4. 4 Cleaner paper trail. At Q2 mid-point review, PDSPT-265 (Q1) and the new ticket (Q2) need to be separable, not entangled.
For Q1 residual · Recommended

Supplement PDSPT-265

Ask Louise to add *_apr columns for the existing 466 Q1 participants. No new participant scan needed — fastest route to the first-week-of-May deliverable.

For Q1 residual · Alternative

Wait for the 30 June pull

Defer until the lot calculation period closes; one consolidated pull covers everything. Slower, but a single clean handoff.
